# Rocky Mountain Growler Drivetrain Repair and Upgrade

## Purpose

Document the reasoning, decisions, and parts purchases related to a drivetrain failure on my Rocky Mountain Growler. The goal was to restore the bike quickly while also positioning the drivetrain for a future upgrade to a **DT Swiss 350 rear wheel**.

---

# Problem

While climbing on a mountain bike ride, the drivetrain suddenly failed.

Symptoms:

- Pedals rotated forward
- Rear wheel did **not engage**
- Sound: **grinding / popping**, similar to a missed gear shift

This strongly suggested **freehub pawl failure**.

Given that my brother has broken two similar hubs in the past year, I wanted to both:

1. **Fix the immediate problem**
2. **Reduce the risk of being stranded in remote terrain**

This concern is especially relevant for riding in Colorado where rides may occur:

- far from trailheads
- at elevation
- deep in mountain terrain

---

# Initial Diagnosis

Original drivetrain setup:

```
Hub: Shimano MT410
Freehub: HG (Hyperglide)
Cassette: SunRace (11T smallest cog)
Drivetrain: 12 speed
```

HG freehubs use a traditional spline interface and require an **11T smallest cog**.

Newer Shimano 12-speed systems use **Microspline**, which allows a **10T smallest cog**.

---

# Strategic Decision

Rather than simply replacing the HG freehub, I chose to **convert the drivetrain to Microspline now**.

Reasons:

1. Enables use of **Shimano Hyperglide+ cassettes**
2. Slightly larger gear range (10–51 vs 11–51)
3. Aligns drivetrain with **modern MTB hub standards**
4. Prepares the bike for a **future DT Swiss 350 wheel upgrade**
5. Allows the cassette to transfer directly to the future wheel

This decision slightly increased cost now but prevents buying drivetrain components twice later.

# Final Tool Setup

The drivetrain work now requires:

```
FR-5.2 cassette lockring tool
chain whip
1" wrench or 1/2" ratchet
10 mm hex key
master link pliers
```

These tools will support most future drivetrain maintenance.

---

# Future Upgrade Path

This upgrade intentionally prepares the bike for a future wheel upgrade.

Planned future component:

```
DT Swiss 350 rear hub / wheel
```

Advantages:

- star ratchet engagement system
- higher durability under torque
- better reliability for remote rides
- tool-free hub service

Because the drivetrain is now **Microspline**, the SLX cassette will transfer directly to the DT Swiss hub.
