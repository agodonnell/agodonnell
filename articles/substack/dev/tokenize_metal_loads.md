# Tokenizing Recycled Metal Loads for Efficient Trade and Settlement

## Overview

The recycled metals market is fragmented, opaque, and heavily reliant on trust between buyers and sellers. Loads of processed metal—categorized using standards like ISRI (e.g., #2 Copper, Copper Chops)—are often moved through informal channels, with limited transparency or recourse when disputes arise over quality or payment terms.

This concept proposes the **tokenization of recycled metal loads**, embedding metadata (e.g., ISRI classification, weight, grade, purity, and location) into a digital token that can be traded, settled, and verified on-chain. A trust and reputation system would support counterparty evaluation and help mitigate risk.

---

## Why This Matters

- **Sellers** want more buy options and faster access to working capital.
- **Buyers** want more seller choices with verified quality history.
- **Logistics partners** want transparency on scheduling and destination certainty.
- **Markets** want efficient clearing mechanisms and pricing signals.

Today, each party operates on trust, manual agreements, and limited visibility. By digitizing loads and building a reputation layer, we enable secure, auditable commerce and potentially unlock new liquidity pathways.

---

## How It Works

1. **Token Creation**  
   Sellers mint a token representing a physical load of recycled metal. Token includes metadata:
   - ISRI code & material description
   - Weight and grade (e.g., 99.99% copper)
   - Source facility and geographic info
   - Timestamp and unique load ID

2. **Reputation Layer**  
   Both buyers and sellers accumulate on-chain reputations based on:
   - Delivery acceptance rates
   - Match between advertised vs received loads
   - Payment reliability and dispute frequency
   - Volume traded

3. **Stablecoin-Based Escrow Settlement**  
   Trade is finalized when buyer locks stablecoins into smart-contract escrow. Settlement is triggered by:
   - Delivery confirmation (e.g., GPS, warehouse scan)
   - Optional oracle validation of grade/purity

4. **Resale and Trading Flexibility**  
   Loads not yet en route can be re-listed or traded again in response to:
   - Mill capacity changes
   - Equipment outages
   - Shifts in copper futures pricing

---

## Use Cases

- **Trusted Load Matching**: Small recyclers can attract large buyers by leveraging on-chain reputation.
- **Flexible Resale Markets**: Sellers can offload booked loads when market dynamics change.
- **Payment Assurance**: Stablecoin escrow protects both sides and reduces friction in high-value trades.
- **Supply Chain Visibility**: Downstream buyers can trace source and quality history of recycled inputs.
